## What Corlang is

Corlang is a language-learning app: a fixed, day-by-day course with spaced-repetition review,
built to prepare you for real use of a language and for the official exams that go with it.

**It prepares you for exams. It does not award anything.** No certificate, qualification, or
proof of level comes from Corlang. Only the official examining body can give you that. Our mock
exams follow the published format and syllabus as closely as we can, and passing one inside the
app is practice, not a result.

## No account

There is no sign-up, no login and no password. Your progress lives on your device. That has a
consequence worth knowing: if you uninstall without exporting a backup, your progress is gone and
we cannot recover it, because we never had it. Use Profile, then Backup, before you switch phones.

## Fair use of the AI tutor

The tutor sends what you type to a language model, which costs real money to answer, so the
subscription includes a daily message allowance. The app shows you how many messages you have
left, and the allowance resets each day.

The tutor is a language tutor and nothing else. It has no access to the internet, no current
information, and it will decline questions outside language learning.

**Language models make mistakes.** The tutor can be confidently wrong about grammar, a word, or a
fact. Treat it as a patient practice partner, not as an authority, and never rely on it for
anything that matters, including anything to do with an exam, a visa, or an application.
